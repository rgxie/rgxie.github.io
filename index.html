Rengan Xie 谢仁淦

I am a Research Assistant Professor at State Key Lab of CAD&CG, Zhejiang University. My main research areas include 3D reconstruction, AIGC, neural rendering, XR, and data visualization. Prior to this role, I received my PhD from the State Key Lab of CAD&CG, Zhejiang University, where I was advised by Prof. Wei Chen and Associate Professor Wenting Zheng.
